### 2026-0038 (Hoekstra): no

- **Proposed:** dispute, € 29,17 incl. VAT
- **Decided by:** Article 4.2

> **Article 4.2 Indexering (Indexation)**
>
> "De prijzen worden per 1 januari 2026 eenmalig geïndexeerd met 3,2 procent, afgerond op hele centen per eenheid. Bepalend is de datum van uitvoering van het werk, niet de factuurdatum. Werk uitgevoerd tot en met 31 december 2025 wordt gefactureerd tegen de prijzen van 2025."
>
> *As of 1 January 2026, the prices are indexed once by 3.2 percent, rounded to whole cents per unit. The date on which the work was carried out is decisive, not the invoice date. Work carried out up to and including 31 December 2025 is invoiced at the 2025 prices.*

The invoice is dated 12 January 2026, so Sanne checked the prices against the new (2026) price list, and they match it. But 4.2 makes the **execution date** decisive, not the invoice date: the work was done on 17 December 2025, so the 2025 prices apply.

- Line 2 (Arbeid installateur): billed € 181,11, at 2025 prices € 175,50 → € 6,79 incl. VAT

Together: **€ 29,17 incl. VAT** to dispute.

The fee billed and disputed is € 46,44, which is € 56,19 incl. VAT.

Difference: Sanne € 54,45, proposed **€ 56,19**.

### DW26-009 (De Wit): no

- **Sanne:** dispute; note: "meerwerk 385,00 boven de 250, geen akkoord gezien" (extra work 385.00 is above the 250, no approval seen)
- **Proposed:** approve, € 0,00 incl. VAT
- **Decided by:** Article 4.5

> **Article 4.5 Meerwerk (Extra work)**
>
> "Werk buiten de werkorder wordt op de factuur apart vermeld als meerwerk. Meerwerk tot en met EUR 250,00 exclusief btw per werkorder mag zonder voorafgaand akkoord worden uitgevoerd. Boven dat bedrag is voorafgaand schriftelijk akkoord nodig, vastgelegd in de werkorder met een nummer GA-xxxx en een goedgekeurd bedrag. Zonder akkoord wordt het meerwerk boven EUR 250,00 niet vergoed, ook niet het deel tot EUR 250,00. Met akkoord wordt ten hoogste het goedgekeurde bedrag vergoed."
>
> *Work outside the work order is listed separately on the invoice as meerwerk (extra work). Meerwerk up to and including EUR 250.00 excluding VAT per work order may be carried out without prior approval. Above that amount, prior written approval is required, recorded in the work order with a number GA-xxxx and an approved amount. Without approval, meerwerk above EUR 250.00 is not reimbursed, not even the part up to EUR 250.00. With approval, at most the approved amount is reimbursed.*

Sanne saw meerwerk of € 385,00, above the € 250,00 that needs no approval, and found no approval. The work-order export does have one: WO-26-1006 has **GA-0412 for € 400,00**, which covers the € 385,00 billed. Under 4.5 it is paid in full, so we propose to approve.

**A second misunderstanding, moot here.** In the audit call (3 Feb 2026, translated from Dutch) Sanne said: *"I think if they don't have it, we only pay the 250?"* Article 4.5 says the opposite: without approval, nothing is paid: "Zonder akkoord wordt het meerwerk boven EUR 250,00 niet vergoed, ook niet het deel tot EUR 250,00." (*Without approval, meerwerk above EUR 250.00 is not reimbursed, not even the part up to EUR 250.00.*). It makes no difference on DW26-009 because GA-0412 covers the amount, but on an invoice without approval she would pay € 250,00 that isn't owed. This month's F26-0024 is that case: meerwerk of € 310,00 without a GA approval, none of it payable.

- **5 approvals we agree with:** 2026-0031, DW26-004, F26-0007, F26-0012, B-26-0097.

- **2026-0044:** the right rule (Article 4.4): 8 labour hours billed, but only 6 are allowed on work order WO-26-1007. Her "8 uur gefactureerd, max 6. 2 uur terugvragen" works out to 2 h × € 60,37 (H02, contract price) = € 120,74 + 21% VAT = € 146,10, exactly the proposed amount.

## What this suggests for Sanne's process

- **She checks the invoice date, not the execution date.** 2026-0038 is dated 12 January 2026 for work done on 17 December 2025; 4.2 makes the execution date decisive. The tool reads the execution date from every invoice.
- **She uses the 2025 call-out price.** € 45,00 instead of the indexed € 46,44. The tool looks up the price that applies on the execution date for every line.
- **She misses approvals in the work-order export.** GA-0412 (€ 400,00) on WO-26-1006 covered DW26-009's meerwerk. In the audit call she said: *"That export is a nightmare, it's one big file."* and *"To be honest, I don't always open it."* The tool looks up every work order.
- **She believes € 250,00 of unapproved meerwerk is paid.** Article 4.5 says nothing is; the tool applies that.

These are exactly the lookups the tool now does for her. Where she disputed and we agree (B-26-0102, 2026-0044, DW26-011), she picked the right rule every time.
